# NX320 SB · SB-34-003 · Rev 01 · standby attitude module — AOA vane connector seal replacement
## Description

This service bulletin introduces the modified standby attitude module retained by the new clamp configuration for the NX-320 navigation and air data.
The change prevents the recurrence of the field-reported condition documented under the Navigation reliability program.
This bulletin belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world use).

## Applicability

Applicable to NX-320 aircraft line numbers 12 through 80 not incorporating modification status MOD-34289.

## Accomplishment Instructions

1. Accomplish the modification during the next scheduled navigation check, or within 1293 flight hours, whichever comes first.
2. Install the modification kit NX-SB34-003-K per the included instruction sheet.
3. Torque the new attach hardware to 42,8 Nm and apply torque seal.
4. Update the aircraft records to reflect the modified standby attitude module retained by the new clamp configuration.
5. Campaign step 01: verify the air data outputs match the test set within the stated tolerance and record the as-found condition on the SB worksheet.
6. Campaign step 02: verify the radio altimeter height reads the bench reference at 30 ft and record the as-found condition on the SB worksheet.
7. Campaign step 03: verify the vane friction stays below the torque-band limit over full travel and record the as-found condition on the SB worksheet.
8. Campaign step 04: verify the radio altimeter height reads the bench reference at 30 ft and record the as-found condition on the SB worksheet.
