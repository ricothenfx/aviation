# NX320 SB · SB-36-004 · Rev 01 · pressure regulating valve — bleed duct clamp collar re-torque campaign
## Description

This service bulletin introduces the modified pressure regulating valve retained by the new clamp configuration for the NX-320 bleed air and pneumatic distribution.
The change prevents the recurrence of the field-reported condition documented under the Pneumatic reliability program.
This bulletin belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world use).

## Applicability

Applicable to NX-320 aircraft line numbers 30 through 92 not incorporating modification status MOD-36555.

## Accomplishment Instructions

1. Accomplish the modification during the next scheduled pneumatic check, or within 424 flight hours, whichever comes first.
2. Install the modification kit NX-SB36-004-K per the included instruction sheet.
3. Torque the new attach hardware to 71,7 Nm and apply torque seal.
4. Update the aircraft records to reflect the modified pressure regulating valve retained by the new clamp configuration.
5. Campaign step 01: verify the the pre-cooler outlet stays below the stated ceiling at max flow and record the as-found condition on the SB worksheet.
6. Campaign step 02: verify the leak detection loop resistance stays inside the pass band and record the as-found condition on the SB worksheet.
7. Campaign step 03: verify the regulated pressure holds the set value across the flow range and record the as-found condition on the SB worksheet.
