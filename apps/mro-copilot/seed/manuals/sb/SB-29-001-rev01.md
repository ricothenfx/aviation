# NX320 SB · SB-29-001 · Rev 01 · electric motor pump (EMP) — reservoir pressurization module fleet improvement
## Description

This service bulletin introduces the modified electric motor pump (EMP) retained by the new clamp configuration for the NX-320 hydraulic power generation and distribution.
The change prevents the recurrence of the field-reported condition documented under the Hydraulic Power reliability program.
This bulletin belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world use).

## Applicability

Applicable to NX-320 aircraft line numbers 40 through 118 not incorporating modification status MOD-29859.

## Accomplishment Instructions

1. Accomplish the modification during the next scheduled hydraulic power check, or within 1320 flight hours, whichever comes first.
2. Install the modification kit NX-SB29-001-K per the included instruction sheet.
3. Torque the new attach hardware to 34,1 Nm and apply torque seal.
4. Update the aircraft records to reflect the modified electric motor pump (EMP) retained by the new clamp configuration.
5. Campaign step 01: verify the no external leakage appears at the module ports after pressurization and record the as-found condition on the SB worksheet.
6. Campaign step 02: verify the system pressure holds 3000 psi with pumps unloaded for 5 min and record the as-found condition on the SB worksheet.
7. Campaign step 03: verify the accumulator gas pre-charge reads within tolerance on the gauge rig and record the as-found condition on the SB worksheet.
