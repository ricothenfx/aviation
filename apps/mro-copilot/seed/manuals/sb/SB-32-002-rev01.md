# NX320 SB · SB-32-002 · Rev 01 · gear actuating cylinder snubber — side stay pin inspection interval extension
## Description

This service bulletin introduces the modified gear actuating cylinder snubber retained by the new clamp configuration for the NX-320 landing gear and steering.
The change prevents the recurrence of the field-reported condition documented under the Landing Gear reliability program.
This bulletin belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world use).

## Applicability

Applicable to NX-320 aircraft line numbers 33 through 92 not incorporating modification status MOD-32443.

## Accomplishment Instructions

1. Accomplish the modification during the next scheduled landing gear check, or within 1993 flight hours, whichever comes first.
2. Install the modification kit NX-SB32-002-K per the included instruction sheet.
3. Torque the new attach hardware to 62,6 Nm and apply torque seal.
4. Update the aircraft records to reflect the modified gear actuating cylinder snubber retained by the new clamp configuration.
5. Campaign step 01: verify the retraction and extension times stay within the acceptance band and record the as-found condition on the SB worksheet.
6. Campaign step 02: verify the uplock rollers show no flat spots under the borescope and record the as-found condition on the SB worksheet.
7. Campaign step 03: verify the steering tiller feedback tracks wheel angle within 1° and record the as-found condition on the SB worksheet.
