# NX320 SB · SB-24-005 · Rev 01 · transformer rectifier unit — TRU cooling fin corrosion protection
## Description

This service bulletin introduces the modified transformer rectifier unit retained by the new clamp configuration for the NX-320 electrical power generation and distribution.
The change prevents the recurrence of the field-reported condition documented under the Electrical Power reliability program.
This bulletin belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world use).

## Applicability

Applicable to NX-320 aircraft line numbers 37 through 119 not incorporating modification status MOD-24469.

## Accomplishment Instructions

1. Accomplish the modification during the next scheduled electrical power check, or within 1044 flight hours, whichever comes first.
2. Install the modification kit NX-SB24-005-K per the included instruction sheet.
3. Torque the new attach hardware to 40,6 Nm and apply torque seal.
4. Update the aircraft records to reflect the modified transformer rectifier unit retained by the new clamp configuration.
5. Campaign step 01: verify the generator frequency holds 400 Hz ± 2 Hz under load transfer and record the as-found condition on the SB worksheet.
6. Campaign step 02: verify the TRU output ripple stays below the stated limit for 60 s and record the as-found condition on the SB worksheet.
7. Campaign step 03: verify the TRU output ripple stays below the stated limit for 60 s and record the as-found condition on the SB worksheet.
