# NX320 AMM · ATA 32 · 32-35-00 · Task 32-35-00-432-342 · tire pressure sensor unit — replacement
## General

This task card covers the replacement of the tire pressure sensor unit on the NX-320 landing gear and steering (Landing Gear, ATA 32).
The tire pressure sensor unit is installed in the side stay pin attach hardware kit and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
Bench-test operating pressure reference for this card: 2324 bar.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the tire pressure sensor unit:

- gear doors can fall when their hinges are disconnected without support
- the shock absorber is charged to high pressure even with the aircraft on jacks

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-845 pressure gauge rig — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-777 system test set — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-3230-798-C — Halcyon Hydraulics.

### Installation

1. Position the tire pressure sensor unit on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
   WARNING: the shock absorber is charged to high pressure even with the aircraft on jacks
2. Torque the tire pressure sensor unit attach bolts to 76.0 Nm in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the tire pressure sensor unit and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
4. Restore the landing gear and steering circuit supply to the tire pressure sensor unit and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the tire pressure sensor unit mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-3230-798-C).

### Test and Close-up

1. Energize the landing gear and steering test configuration and confirm the tire pressure sensor unit responds to command inputs.
2. Record the reference value steering tracking error 0.8° and compare it with the acceptance band for the tire pressure sensor unit.
   NOTE: Verify the nose wheel steering tiller feedback unit is relieved of stored energy before continuing.
3. Cycle the tire pressure sensor unit through 6 complete operating cycles and watch for hesitation or drift.
4. Leak-check every port and coupling touched during this task with the system at operating pressure.
   CAUTION: Verify the nose landing gear shock absorber is relieved of residual torque before continuing.
5. Monitor the uplock rollers show no flat spots under the borescope during cycle 01 and log any deviation in the task sheet.
6. Monitor the retraction and extension times stay within the acceptance band during cycle 02 and log any deviation in the task sheet.
