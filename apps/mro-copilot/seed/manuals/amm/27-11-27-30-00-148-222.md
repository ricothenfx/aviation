# NX320 AMM · ATA 27 · 27-30-00 · Task 27-30-00-148-222 · pitch trim motor clutch — replacement
## General

This task card covers the replacement of the pitch trim motor clutch on the NX-320 primary and secondary flight controls (Flight Controls, ATA 27).
The pitch trim motor clutch is installed in the flap carriage and track assembly and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
Bench-test operating pressure reference for this card: 2385 bar.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the pitch trim motor clutch:

- hydraulic pressure in the PCU circuit can cause uncommanded surface movement
- control surface mass balance weights must never be loosened without support

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-314 rigging pin kit — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-422 hydraulic test rig — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-2730-820-C — Kepler Avionics.

1. Depressurize and drain the primary and secondary flight controls circuit serving the pitch trim motor clutch, then verify zero pressure on the associated gauge.

### Installation

1. Position the pitch trim motor clutch on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
   WARNING: flap surfaces can drift under their own weight when the tracks are released
2. Torque the pitch trim motor clutch attach bolts to 64.5 Nm in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the pitch trim motor clutch and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
4. Restore the primary and secondary flight controls circuit supply to the pitch trim motor clutch and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the pitch trim motor clutch mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-2730-820-C).
   WARNING: flap surfaces can drift under their own weight when the tracks are released
