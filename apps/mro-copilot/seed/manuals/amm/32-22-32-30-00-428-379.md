# NX320 AMM · ATA 32 · 32-30-00 · Task 32-30-00-428-379 · landing gear uplock assembly — inspection and check
## General

This task card covers the inspection and check of the landing gear uplock assembly on the NX-320 landing gear and steering (Landing Gear, ATA 32).
The landing gear uplock assembly is installed in the uplock roller and spring kit and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
Bench-test operating pressure reference for this card: 2877 bar.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the landing gear uplock assembly:

- the shock absorber is charged to high pressure even with the aircraft on jacks
- an unsecured uplock can release the gear under its own weight

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-508 bonding meter — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-845 pressure gauge rig — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-3285-682-C — Halcyon Hydraulics.

### Installation

1. Position the landing gear uplock assembly on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
2. Torque the landing gear uplock assembly attach bolts to 45.0 Nm in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
   NOTE: Verify the nose landing gear shock absorber is relieved of stored energy before continuing.

3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the landing gear uplock assembly and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
4. Restore the landing gear and steering circuit supply to the landing gear uplock assembly and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the landing gear uplock assembly mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-3285-682-C).

### Test and Close-up

1. Energize the landing gear and steering test configuration and confirm the landing gear uplock assembly responds to command inputs.
2. Record the reference value extension time 11.5 s and compare it with the acceptance band for the landing gear uplock assembly.
3. Cycle the landing gear uplock assembly through 5 complete operating cycles and watch for hesitation or drift.
4. Leak-check every port and coupling touched during this task with the system at operating pressure.
5. Monitor the retraction and extension times stay within the acceptance band during cycle 01 and log any deviation in the task sheet.
