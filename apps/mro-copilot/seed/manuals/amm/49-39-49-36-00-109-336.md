# NX320 AMM · ATA 49 · 49-36-00 · Task 49-36-00-109-336 · APU start contactor — operational test
## General

This task card covers the operational test of the APU start contactor on the NX-320 auxiliary power unit (Auxiliary Power Unit, ATA 49).
The APU start contactor is installed in the APU fuel metering valve coupling kit and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the APU start contactor:

- APU starter torque can injure hands left near the ring gear
- the intake door can close under gravity when its actuator is disconnected

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-210 torque wrench set — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-422 hydraulic test rig — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-4964-376-C — Kepler Avionics.

### Installation

1. Position the APU start contactor on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
   NOTE: the APU compartment stays hot enough to ignite residual fuel vapour for 15 min after shutdown
2. Torque the APU start contactor attach bolts to 27.5 Nm in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
   CAUTION: Verify the APU start contactor is relieved of power before continuing.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the APU start contactor and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
   NOTE: the intake door can close under gravity when its actuator is disconnected
4. Restore the auxiliary power unit circuit supply to the APU start contactor and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
   NOTE: Verify the load compressor surge control valve is relieved of power before continuing.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the APU start contactor mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-4964-376-C).
6. Close the access panel and remove the warning placards once the APU start contactor is secured.
   CAUTION: Verify the APU start contactor is relieved of power before continuing.
7. Reinstall the sensor line at position 01 of the APU start contactor installation; torque the coupling per the standard practice table.

### Test and Close-up

1. Energize the auxiliary power unit test configuration and confirm the APU start contactor responds to command inputs.
2. Record the reference value idle oil pressure 58 psi and compare it with the acceptance band for the APU start contactor.
3. Cycle the APU start contactor through 6 complete operating cycles and watch for hesitation or drift.
4. Leak-check every port and coupling touched during this task with the system at operating pressure.
5. Monitor the EGT stays inside the start curve during a dry motoring check during cycle 01 and log any deviation in the task sheet.
