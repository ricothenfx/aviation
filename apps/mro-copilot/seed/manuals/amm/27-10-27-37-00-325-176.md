# NX320 AMM · ATA 27 · 27-37-00 · Task 27-37-00-325-176 · spoiler mixer unit — inspection and check
## General

This task card covers the inspection and check of the spoiler mixer unit on the NX-320 primary and secondary flight controls (Flight Controls, ATA 27).
The spoiler mixer unit is installed in the flap carriage and track assembly and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the spoiler mixer unit:

- control surface mass balance weights must never be loosened without support
- flap surfaces can drift under their own weight when the tracks are released

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-422 hydraulic test rig — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-210 torque wrench set — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-2774-247-C — Kepler Avionics.

### Installation

1. Position the spoiler mixer unit on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
   NOTE: hydraulic pressure in the PCU circuit can cause uncommanded surface movement
2. Torque the spoiler mixer unit attach bolts to 48.5 lbf·ft in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
   CAUTION: Verify the aileron PCU is relieved of power before continuing.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the spoiler mixer unit and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
   NOTE: flap surfaces can drift under their own weight when the tracks are released
4. Restore the primary and secondary flight controls circuit supply to the spoiler mixer unit and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
   NOTE: Verify the rudder trim actuator is relieved of power before continuing.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the spoiler mixer unit mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-2774-247-C).
6. Close the access panel and remove the warning placards once the spoiler mixer unit is secured.
   CAUTION: Verify the aileron PCU is relieved of power before continuing.
7. Reinstall the bracket at position 01 of the spoiler mixer unit installation; torque the coupling per the standard practice table.

### Test and Close-up

1. Energize the primary and secondary flight controls test configuration and confirm the spoiler mixer unit responds to command inputs.
2. Record the reference value aileron deflection 22.4° and compare it with the acceptance band for the spoiler mixer unit.
3. Cycle the spoiler mixer unit through 3 complete operating cycles and watch for hesitation or drift.
4. Leak-check every port and coupling touched during this task with the system at operating pressure.
