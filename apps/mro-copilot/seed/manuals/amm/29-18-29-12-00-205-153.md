# NX320 AMM · ATA 29 · 29-12-00 · Task 29-12-00-205-153 · electric motor pump (EMP) — replacement
## General

This task card covers the replacement of the electric motor pump (EMP) on the NX-320 hydraulic power generation and distribution (Hydraulic Power, ATA 29).
The electric motor pump (EMP) is installed in the EDP drive shaft coupling and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the electric motor pump (EMP):

- the accumulator pre-charge side stays pressurized with the system depressurized
- residual pressure in the high-pressure manifold can inject fluid through the skin

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-777 system test set — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-630 borescope — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-2928-931-C — Kepler Avionics.

### Installation

1. Position the electric motor pump (EMP) on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
   NOTE: residual pressure in the high-pressure manifold can inject fluid through the skin
2. Torque the electric motor pump (EMP) attach bolts to 68.5 Nm in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
   CAUTION: Verify the hydraulic reservoir pressurization module is relieved of power before continuing.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the electric motor pump (EMP) and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
   NOTE: Skydrol rapidly degrades eye tissue — goggles are mandatory
4. Restore the hydraulic power generation and distribution circuit supply to the electric motor pump (EMP) and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
   NOTE: Verify the electric motor pump (EMP) is relieved of power before continuing.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the electric motor pump (EMP) mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-2928-931-C).
