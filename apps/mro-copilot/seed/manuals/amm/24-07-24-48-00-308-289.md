# NX320 AMM · ATA 24 · 24-48-00 · Task 24-48-00-308-289 · generator control unit — operational test
## General

This task card covers the operational test of the generator control unit on the NX-320 electrical power generation and distribution (Electrical Power, ATA 24).
The generator control unit is installed in the primary power distribution rack and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
Bench-test operating pressure reference for this card: 1664 bar.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the generator control unit:

- battery terminals are live even with the battery switch OFF
- arc-flash PPE is mandatory before opening loaded bus tie panels

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-630 borescope — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-314 rigging pin kit — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-2446-645-B — Nordwind Aero Supply.

### Installation

1. Position the generator control unit on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
   WARNING: battery terminals are live even with the battery switch OFF
2. Torque the generator control unit attach bolts to 70.0 Nm in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the generator control unit and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
4. Restore the electrical power generation and distribution circuit supply to the generator control unit and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the generator control unit mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-2446-645-B).
   WARNING: battery terminals are live even with the battery switch OFF
6. Close the access panel and remove the warning placards once the generator control unit is secured.

### Test and Close-up

1. Energize the electrical power generation and distribution test configuration and confirm the generator control unit responds to command inputs.
2. Record the reference value generator load 38 kVA and compare it with the acceptance band for the generator control unit.
   NOTE: Verify the static inverter is relieved of stored energy before continuing.
3. Cycle the generator control unit through 5 complete operating cycles and watch for hesitation or drift.
4. Leak-check every port and coupling touched during this task with the system at operating pressure.
   CAUTION: Verify the variable speed constant frequency generator is relieved of residual torque before continuing.
5. Monitor the TRU output ripple stays below the stated limit for 60 s during cycle 01 and log any deviation in the task sheet.
6. Monitor the generator frequency holds 400 Hz ± 2 Hz under load transfer during cycle 02 and log any deviation in the task sheet.
7. Monitor the battery contactor clicks once, without chattering, on engage during cycle 03 and log any deviation in the task sheet.
8. Monitor the generator frequency holds 400 Hz ± 2 Hz under load transfer during cycle 04 and log any deviation in the task sheet.
