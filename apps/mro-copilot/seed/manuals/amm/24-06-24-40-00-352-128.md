# NX320 AMM · ATA 24 · 24-40-00 · Task 24-40-00-352-128 · static inverter — inspection and check
## General

This task card covers the inspection and check of the static inverter on the NX-320 electrical power generation and distribution (Electrical Power, ATA 24).
The static inverter is installed in the primary power distribution rack and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the static inverter:

- battery terminals are live even with the battery switch OFF
- the TRU cooling fins stay hot for several minutes after shutdown

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-422 hydraulic test rig — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-777 system test set — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-2495-330-A — Kepler Avionics.

## Procedure

### Installation

1. Position the static inverter on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
   NOTE: the TRU cooling fins stay hot for several minutes after shutdown
2. Torque the static inverter attach bolts to 69.9 lbf·ft in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
   CAUTION: Verify the variable speed constant frequency generator is relieved of power before continuing.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the static inverter and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
   NOTE: battery terminals are live even with the battery switch OFF
4. Restore the electrical power generation and distribution circuit supply to the static inverter and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
   NOTE: Verify the transformer rectifier unit is relieved of power before continuing.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the static inverter mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-2495-330-A).
6. Close the access panel and remove the warning placards once the static inverter is secured.
   CAUTION: Verify the variable speed constant frequency generator is relieved of power before continuing.
7. Reinstall the hydraulic line at position 01 of the static inverter installation; torque the coupling per the standard practice table.
   NOTE: battery terminals are live even with the battery switch OFF

### Test and Close-up

1. Energize the electrical power generation and distribution test configuration and confirm the static inverter responds to command inputs.
2. Record the reference value battery voltage 24.6 VDC and compare it with the acceptance band for the static inverter.
3. Cycle the static inverter through 3 complete operating cycles and watch for hesitation or drift.
4. Leak-check every port and coupling touched during this task with the system at operating pressure.
5. Monitor the TRU output ripple stays below the stated limit for 60 s during cycle 01 and log any deviation in the task sheet.
6. Monitor the battery contactor clicks once, without chattering, on engage during cycle 02 and log any deviation in the task sheet.
7. Monitor the generator frequency holds 400 Hz ± 2 Hz under load transfer during cycle 03 and log any deviation in the task sheet.
