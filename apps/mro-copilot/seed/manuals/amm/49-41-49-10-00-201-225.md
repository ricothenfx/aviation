# NX320 AMM · ATA 49 · 49-10-00 · Task 49-10-00-201-225 · load compressor surge control valve — replacement
## General

This task card covers the replacement of the load compressor surge control valve on the NX-320 auxiliary power unit (Auxiliary Power Unit, ATA 49).
The load compressor surge control valve is installed in the APU fuel metering valve coupling kit and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the load compressor surge control valve:

- the intake door can close under gravity when its actuator is disconnected
- APU starter torque can injure hands left near the ring gear

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-630 borescope — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-845 pressure gauge rig — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-4967-762-A — Halcyon Hydraulics.

## Procedure

1. Depressurize and drain the auxiliary power unit circuit serving the load compressor surge control valve, then verify zero pressure on the associated gauge.
2. Attach the applicable warning placards and open the access panel adjacent to the load compressor surge control valve.

   NOTE: the APU compartment stays hot enough to ignite residual fuel vapour for 15 min after shutdown
4. Support the load compressor surge control valve with a suitable sling before loosening any attach hardware.

### Installation

1. Position the load compressor surge control valve on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
2. Torque the load compressor surge control valve attach bolts to 29.0 Nm in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the load compressor surge control valve and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
4. Restore the auxiliary power unit circuit supply to the load compressor surge control valve and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
   NOTE: Verify the EBU bleed air shut-off valve is relieved of residual torque before continuing.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the load compressor surge control valve mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-4967-762-A).

### Test and Close-up

1. Energize the auxiliary power unit test configuration and confirm the load compressor surge control valve responds to command inputs.
2. Record the reference value intake door open time 6.5 s and compare it with the acceptance band for the load compressor surge control valve.
3. Cycle the load compressor surge control valve through 3 complete operating cycles and watch for hesitation or drift.
   WARNING: APU starter torque can injure hands left near the ring gear
4. Leak-check every port and coupling touched during this task with the system at operating pressure.
   NOTE: Verify the APU start contactor is relieved of stored energy before continuing.
5. Monitor the oil pressure reaches the idle floor within the stated seconds during cycle 01 and log any deviation in the task sheet.
