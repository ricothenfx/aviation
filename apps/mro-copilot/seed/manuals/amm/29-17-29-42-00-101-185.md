# NX320 AMM · ATA 29 · 29-42-00 · Task 29-42-00-101-185 · power transfer unit — operational test
## General

This task card covers the operational test of the power transfer unit on the NX-320 hydraulic power generation and distribution (Hydraulic Power, ATA 29).
The power transfer unit is installed in the EDP drive shaft coupling and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the power transfer unit:

- Skydrol rapidly degrades eye tissue — goggles are mandatory
- residual pressure in the high-pressure manifold can inject fluid through the skin

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-630 borescope — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-911 generator load bank — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-2954-965-B — Kepler Avionics.

### Installation

1. Position the power transfer unit on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
2. Torque the power transfer unit attach bolts to 41.8 lbf·ft in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
   NOTE: Verify the hydraulic reservoir pressurization module is relieved of stored energy before continuing.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the power transfer unit and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
4. Restore the hydraulic power generation and distribution circuit supply to the power transfer unit and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the power transfer unit mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-2954-965-B).
6. Close the access panel and remove the warning placards once the power transfer unit is secured.
7. Reinstall the sensor line at position 01 of the power transfer unit installation; torque the coupling per the standard practice table.
