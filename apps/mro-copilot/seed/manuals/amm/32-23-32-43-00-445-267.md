# NX320 AMM · ATA 32 · 32-43-00 · Task 32-43-00-445-267 · main gear side stay pin — operational test
## General

This task card covers the operational test of the main gear side stay pin on the NX-320 landing gear and steering (Landing Gear, ATA 32).
The main gear side stay pin is installed in the uplock roller and spring kit and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the main gear side stay pin:

- an unsecured uplock can release the gear under its own weight
- gear doors can fall when their hinges are disconnected without support

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-845 pressure gauge rig — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-911 generator load bank — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-3251-286-A — Kepler Avionics.

### Installation

1. Position the main gear side stay pin on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
   NOTE: gear doors can fall when their hinges are disconnected without support
2. Torque the main gear side stay pin attach bolts to 20.4 lbf·ft in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
   CAUTION: Verify the nose landing gear shock absorber is relieved of power before continuing.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the main gear side stay pin and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
   NOTE: the shock absorber is charged to high pressure even with the aircraft on jacks
4. Restore the landing gear and steering circuit supply to the main gear side stay pin and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
   NOTE: Verify the landing gear uplock assembly is relieved of power before continuing.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the main gear side stay pin mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-3251-286-A).

### Test and Close-up

1. Energize the landing gear and steering test configuration and confirm the main gear side stay pin responds to command inputs.
2. Record the reference value steering tracking error 0.8° and compare it with the acceptance band for the main gear side stay pin.
3. Cycle the main gear side stay pin through 3 complete operating cycles and watch for hesitation or drift.
4. Leak-check every port and coupling touched during this task with the system at operating pressure.
5. Monitor the uplock rollers show no flat spots under the borescope during cycle 01 and log any deviation in the task sheet.
