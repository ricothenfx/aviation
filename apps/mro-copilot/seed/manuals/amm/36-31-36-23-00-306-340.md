# NX320 AMM · ATA 36 · 36-23-00 · Task 36-23-00-306-340 · cross-bleed valve actuator — inspection and check
## General

This task card covers the inspection and check of the cross-bleed valve actuator on the NX-320 bleed air and pneumatic distribution (Pneumatic, ATA 36).
The cross-bleed valve actuator is installed in the bleed duct clamp collar set and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the cross-bleed valve actuator:

- duct clamp collars hold stored spring energy when loosened unevenly
- bleed ducts carry 200 °C air at pressure — burns and injection injuries are likely

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-911 generator load bank — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-314 rigging pin kit — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-3617-282-C — Nordwind Aero Supply.

### Installation

1. Position the cross-bleed valve actuator on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
   NOTE: bleed ducts carry 200 °C air at pressure — burns and injection injuries are likely
2. Torque the cross-bleed valve actuator attach bolts to 11.1 lbf·ft in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
   CAUTION: Verify the bleed pre-cooler control valve is relieved of power before continuing.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the cross-bleed valve actuator and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
4. Restore the bleed air and pneumatic distribution circuit supply to the cross-bleed valve actuator and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the cross-bleed valve actuator mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-3617-282-C).

### Test and Close-up

2. Record the reference value pre-cooler outlet 178 °C and compare it with the acceptance band for the cross-bleed valve actuator.
3. Cycle the cross-bleed valve actuator through 6 complete operating cycles and watch for hesitation or drift.
4. Leak-check every port and coupling touched during this task with the system at operating pressure.
