# NX320 AMM · ATA 28 · 28-24-00 · Task 28-24-00-416-114 · center tank fuel pump — operational test
## General

This task card covers the operational test of the center tank fuel pump on the NX-320 fuel storage and distribution (Fuel, ATA 28).
The center tank fuel pump is installed in the fuel pump inlet strainer housing and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the center tank fuel pump:

- residual fuel keeps dripping from couplings for several minutes
- fuel vapour in the tank volume is explosive at ordinary temperatures

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-845 pressure gauge rig — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-911 generator load bank — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-2842-102-A — Nordwind Aero Supply.

### Installation

1. Position the center tank fuel pump on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
   NOTE: fuel vapour in the tank volume is explosive at ordinary temperatures
2. Torque the center tank fuel pump attach bolts to 74.5 Nm in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
   CAUTION: Verify the center tank fuel pump is relieved of power before continuing.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the center tank fuel pump and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
   NOTE: bonding leads must connect before any fuel line is broken
4. Restore the fuel storage and distribution circuit supply to the center tank fuel pump and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
   NOTE: Verify the refuel/defuel selector valve is relieved of power before continuing.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the center tank fuel pump mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-2842-102-A).
6. Close the access panel and remove the warning placards once the center tank fuel pump is secured.
