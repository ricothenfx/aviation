# NX320 AMM · ATA 36 · 36-19-00 · Task 36-19-00-363-200 · duct leak detection loop — inspection and check
## General

This task card covers the inspection and check of the duct leak detection loop on the NX-320 bleed air and pneumatic distribution (Pneumatic, ATA 36).
The duct leak detection loop is installed in the bleed duct clamp collar set and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the duct leak detection loop:

- the overpressure switch resets only below 15 psi line pressure
- duct clamp collars hold stored spring energy when loosened unevenly

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-422 hydraulic test rig — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-777 system test set — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-3696-190-C — Nordwind Aero Supply.

### Installation

1. Position the duct leak detection loop on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
2. Torque the duct leak detection loop attach bolts to 29.6 lbf·ft in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
   NOTE: Verify the bleed pre-cooler control valve is relieved of stored energy before continuing.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the duct leak detection loop and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
4. Restore the bleed air and pneumatic distribution circuit supply to the duct leak detection loop and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the duct leak detection loop mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-3696-190-C).
6. Close the access panel and remove the warning placards once the duct leak detection loop is secured.
7. Reinstall the vent line at position 01 of the duct leak detection loop installation; torque the coupling per the standard practice table.

### Test and Close-up

2. Record the reference value pre-cooler outlet 178 °C and compare it with the acceptance band for the duct leak detection loop.

4. Leak-check every port and coupling touched during this task with the system at operating pressure.
