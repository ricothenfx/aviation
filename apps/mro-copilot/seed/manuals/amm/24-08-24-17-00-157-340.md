# NX320 AMM · ATA 24 · 24-17-00 · Task 24-17-00-157-340 · variable speed constant frequency generator — adjustment and rigging
## General

This task card covers the adjustment and rigging of the variable speed constant frequency generator on the NX-320 electrical power generation and distribution (Electrical Power, ATA 24).
The variable speed constant frequency generator is installed in the primary power distribution rack and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the variable speed constant frequency generator:

- arc-flash PPE is mandatory before opening loaded bus tie panels
- battery terminals are live even with the battery switch OFF

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-845 pressure gauge rig — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-210 torque wrench set — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-2426-616-B — Halcyon Hydraulics.

### Installation

1. Position the variable speed constant frequency generator on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.

<!-- page: 129 -->
2. Torque the variable speed constant frequency generator attach bolts to 51.4 lbf·ft in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the variable speed constant frequency generator and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
4. Restore the electrical power generation and distribution circuit supply to the variable speed constant frequency generator and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
   NOTE: Verify the main battery contactor is relieved of residual torque before continuing.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the variable speed constant frequency generator mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-2426-616-B).
6. Close the access panel and remove the warning placards once the variable speed constant frequency generator is secured.

### Test and Close-up

1. Energize the electrical power generation and distribution test configuration and confirm the variable speed constant frequency generator responds to command inputs.

<!-- page: 135 -->
2. Record the reference value battery voltage 24.6 VDC and compare it with the acceptance band for the variable speed constant frequency generator.
3. Cycle the variable speed constant frequency generator through 6 complete operating cycles and watch for hesitation or drift.
   WARNING: arc-flash PPE is mandatory before opening loaded bus tie panels
4. Leak-check every port and coupling touched during this task with the system at operating pressure.
   NOTE: Verify the variable speed constant frequency generator is relieved of stored energy before continuing.
