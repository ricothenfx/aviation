# NX320 AMM · ATA 49 · 49-19-00 · Task 49-19-00-249-251 · APU intake door actuator — inspection and check
## General

This task card covers the inspection and check of the APU intake door actuator on the NX-320 auxiliary power unit (Auxiliary Power Unit, ATA 49).
The APU intake door actuator is installed in the APU fuel metering valve coupling kit and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
Bench-test operating pressure reference for this card: 1476 bar.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the APU intake door actuator:

- the APU compartment stays hot enough to ignite residual fuel vapour for 15 min after shutdown
- APU starter torque can injure hands left near the ring gear

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-630 borescope — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-777 system test set — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-4997-757-B — Meridian Gear Co..

### Installation

1. Position the APU intake door actuator on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
   WARNING: the intake door can close under gravity when its actuator is disconnected
2. Torque the APU intake door actuator attach bolts to 64.0 Nm in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the APU intake door actuator and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
4. Restore the auxiliary power unit circuit supply to the APU intake door actuator and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the APU intake door actuator mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-4997-757-B).

### Test and Close-up

1. Energize the auxiliary power unit test configuration and confirm the APU intake door actuator responds to command inputs.
2. Record the reference value idle oil pressure 58 psi and compare it with the acceptance band for the APU intake door actuator.
   NOTE: Verify the APU oil pressure transmitter is relieved of stored energy before continuing.
3. Cycle the APU intake door actuator through 5 complete operating cycles and watch for hesitation or drift.
4. Leak-check every port and coupling touched during this task with the system at operating pressure.
   CAUTION: Verify the APU start contactor is relieved of residual torque before continuing.
5. Monitor the oil pressure reaches the idle floor within the stated seconds during cycle 01 and log any deviation in the task sheet.
6. Monitor the oil pressure reaches the idle floor within the stated seconds during cycle 02 and log any deviation in the task sheet.
