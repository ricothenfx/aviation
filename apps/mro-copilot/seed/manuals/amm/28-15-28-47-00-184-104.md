# NX320 AMM · ATA 28 · 28-47-00 · Task 28-47-00-184-104 · wing tank water scavenger system — removal and installation
## General

This task card covers the removal and installation of the wing tank water scavenger system on the NX-320 fuel storage and distribution (Fuel, ATA 28).
The wing tank water scavenger system is installed in the refuel gallery coupling assembly and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the wing tank water scavenger system:

- fuel vapour in the tank volume is explosive at ordinary temperatures
- residual fuel keeps dripping from couplings for several minutes

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-422 hydraulic test rig — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-422 hydraulic test rig — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-2885-796-C — Meridian Gear Co..

### Installation

1. Position the wing tank water scavenger system on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
   WARNING: bonding leads must connect before any fuel line is broken
2. Torque the wing tank water scavenger system attach bolts to 51.5 Nm in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the wing tank water scavenger system and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
4. Restore the fuel storage and distribution circuit supply to the wing tank water scavenger system and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the wing tank water scavenger system mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-2885-796-C).
