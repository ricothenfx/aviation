# NX320 AMM · ATA 21 · 21-15-00 · Task 21-15-00-232-204 · re-circulation fan — removal and installation
## General

This task card covers the removal and installation of the re-circulation fan on the NX-320 air conditioning and pressurization (Air Conditioning, ATA 21).
The re-circulation fan is installed in the conditioned air distribution manifold and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
Bench-test operating pressure reference for this card: 2476 bar.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the re-circulation fan:

- sudden cabin pressure changes can dislodge unsecured panels
- pack bay reaches flight-idle temperatures within seconds of pack start

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-314 rigging pin kit — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-630 borescope — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-2135-732-C — Halcyon Hydraulics.

## Procedure

### Removal

1. Depressurize and drain the air conditioning and pressurization circuit serving the re-circulation fan, then verify zero pressure on the associated gauge.
2. Attach the applicable warning placards and open the access panel adjacent to the re-circulation fan.
   NOTE: Verify the water extractor is relieved of power before continuing.
3. Disconnect the electrical connector of the re-circulation fan and cap the receptacle with a protective cover.
4. Support the re-circulation fan with a suitable sling before loosening any attach hardware.

### Installation

1. Position the re-circulation fan on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
   WARNING: conditioned air ducts can exceed 120 °C during bleed operations
2. Torque the re-circulation fan attach bolts to 68.0 Nm in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the re-circulation fan and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
4. Restore the air conditioning and pressurization circuit supply to the re-circulation fan and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the re-circulation fan mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-2135-732-C).
   WARNING: conditioned air ducts can exceed 120 °C during bleed operations
6. Close the access panel and remove the warning placards once the re-circulation fan is secured.
7. Reinstall the clamp collar at position 01 of the re-circulation fan installation; torque the coupling per the standard practice table.

### Test and Close-up

1. Energize the air conditioning and pressurization test configuration and confirm the re-circulation fan responds to command inputs.
2. Record the reference value pack outlet temperature 8.2 °C and compare it with the acceptance band for the re-circulation fan.

   NOTE: Verify the cabin pressure outflow valve is relieved of stored energy before continuing.
