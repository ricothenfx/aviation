# NX320 AMM · ATA 21 · 21-49-00 · Task 21-49-00-176-315 · water extractor — inspection and check
## General

This task card covers the inspection and check of the water extractor on the NX-320 air conditioning and pressurization (Air Conditioning, ATA 21).
The water extractor is installed in the conditioned air distribution manifold and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
Bench-test operating pressure reference for this card: 1220 bar.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the water extractor:

- conditioned air ducts can exceed 120 °C during bleed operations
- pack bay reaches flight-idle temperatures within seconds of pack start

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-210 torque wrench set — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-630 borescope — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-2189-200-A — Meridian Gear Co..

### Installation

1. Position the water extractor on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
2. Torque the water extractor attach bolts to 54.0 lbf·ft in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the water extractor and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
4. Restore the air conditioning and pressurization circuit supply to the water extractor and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
   NOTE: Verify the temperature trim air valve is relieved of residual torque before continuing.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the water extractor mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-2189-200-A).
6. Close the access panel and remove the warning placards once the water extractor is secured.
7. Reinstall the sensor line at position 01 of the water extractor installation; torque the coupling per the standard practice table.

### Test and Close-up

1. Energize the air conditioning and pressurization test configuration and confirm the water extractor responds to command inputs.
2. Record the reference value cabin Δp 7.8 psi and compare it with the acceptance band for the water extractor.
3. Cycle the water extractor through 3 complete operating cycles and watch for hesitation or drift.
   WARNING: sudden cabin pressure changes can dislodge unsecured panels
4. Leak-check every port and coupling touched during this task with the system at operating pressure.
   NOTE: Verify the pack flow control valve is relieved of stored energy before continuing.
5. Monitor the pack flow indication stabilizes within the commanded band during cycle 01 and log any deviation in the task sheet.
