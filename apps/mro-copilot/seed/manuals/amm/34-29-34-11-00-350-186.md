# NX320 AMM · ATA 34 · 34-11-00 · Task 34-11-00-350-186 · standby attitude module — removal and installation
## General

This task card covers the removal and installation of the standby attitude module on the NX-320 navigation and air data (Navigation, ATA 34).
The standby attitude module is installed in the ADR pitot line disconnect panel and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the standby attitude module:

- AOA vanes are mass-balanced — never rotate one backwards by hand
- the standby battery keeps the attitude module live with all power off

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-777 system test set — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-845 pressure gauge rig — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-3474-446-A — Kepler Avionics.

## Procedure

1. Depressurize and drain the navigation and air data circuit serving the standby attitude module, then verify zero pressure on the associated gauge.
2. Attach the applicable warning placards and open the access panel adjacent to the standby attitude module.
3. Disconnect the electrical connector of the standby attitude module and cap the receptacle with a protective cover.
   NOTE: probe heat reaches burn temperatures within seconds when powered
4. Support the standby attitude module with a suitable sling before loosening any attach hardware.

### Installation

1. Position the standby attitude module on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
2. Torque the standby attitude module attach bolts to 30.5 Nm in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the standby attitude module and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
4. Restore the navigation and air data circuit supply to the standby attitude module and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
   NOTE: Verify the pitot probe heat controller is relieved of residual torque before continuing.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the standby attitude module mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-3474-446-A).
6. Close the access panel and remove the warning placards once the standby attitude module is secured.
7. Reinstall the drain line at position 01 of the standby attitude module installation; torque the coupling per the standard practice table.
   CAUTION: probe heat reaches burn temperatures within seconds when powered

### Test and Close-up

1. Energize the navigation and air data test configuration and confirm the standby attitude module responds to command inputs.
2. Record the reference value pitot heat 241 W and compare it with the acceptance band for the standby attitude module.
3. Cycle the standby attitude module through 5 complete operating cycles and watch for hesitation or drift.
   WARNING: AOA vanes are mass-balanced — never rotate one backwards by hand
4. Leak-check every port and coupling touched during this task with the system at operating pressure.
   NOTE: Verify the air data inertial reference unit is relieved of stored energy before continuing.
5. Monitor the vane friction stays below the torque-band limit over full travel during cycle 01 and log any deviation in the task sheet.
   NOTE: the standby battery keeps the attitude module live with all power off
6. Monitor the radio altimeter height reads the bench reference at 30 ft during cycle 02 and log any deviation in the task sheet.
