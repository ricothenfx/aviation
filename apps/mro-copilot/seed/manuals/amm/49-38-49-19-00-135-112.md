# NX320 AMM · ATA 49 · 49-19-00 · Task 49-19-00-135-112 · EBU bleed air shut-off valve — replacement
## General

This task card covers the replacement of the EBU bleed air shut-off valve on the NX-320 auxiliary power unit (Auxiliary Power Unit, ATA 49).
The EBU bleed air shut-off valve is installed in the APU fuel metering valve coupling kit and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the EBU bleed air shut-off valve:

- APU starter torque can injure hands left near the ring gear
- the APU compartment stays hot enough to ignite residual fuel vapour for 15 min after shutdown

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-911 generator load bank — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-630 borescope — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-4945-543-C — Meridian Gear Co..

### Installation

1. Position the EBU bleed air shut-off valve on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
2. Torque the EBU bleed air shut-off valve attach bolts to 78.0 Nm in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
   NOTE: Verify the APU start contactor is relieved of stored energy before continuing.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the EBU bleed air shut-off valve and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
4. Restore the auxiliary power unit circuit supply to the EBU bleed air shut-off valve and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the EBU bleed air shut-off valve mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-4945-543-C).
