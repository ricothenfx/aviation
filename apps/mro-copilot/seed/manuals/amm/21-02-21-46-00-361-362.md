# NX320 AMM · ATA 21 · 21-46-00 · Task 21-46-00-361-362 · cabin pressure outflow valve — replacement
## General

This task card covers the replacement of the cabin pressure outflow valve on the NX-320 air conditioning and pressurization (Air Conditioning, ATA 21).
The cabin pressure outflow valve is installed in the pack bay ducting assembly and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the cabin pressure outflow valve:

- sudden cabin pressure changes can dislodge unsecured panels
- pack bay reaches flight-idle temperatures within seconds of pack start

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-845 pressure gauge rig — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-422 hydraulic test rig — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-2180-161-B — Halcyon Hydraulics.

### Installation

1. Position the cabin pressure outflow valve on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
   NOTE: pack bay reaches flight-idle temperatures within seconds of pack start
2. Torque the cabin pressure outflow valve attach bolts to 92.5 Nm in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
   CAUTION: Verify the pack flow control valve is relieved of power before continuing.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the cabin pressure outflow valve and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
   NOTE: conditioned air ducts can exceed 120 °C during bleed operations
4. Restore the air conditioning and pressurization circuit supply to the cabin pressure outflow valve and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
   NOTE: Verify the water extractor is relieved of power before continuing.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the cabin pressure outflow valve mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-2180-161-B).
