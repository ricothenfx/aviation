# NX320 AMM · ATA 29 · 29-24-00 · Task 29-24-00-179-323 · hydraulic reservoir pressurization module — inspection and check
## General

This task card covers the inspection and check of the hydraulic reservoir pressurization module on the NX-320 hydraulic power generation and distribution (Hydraulic Power, ATA 29).
The hydraulic reservoir pressurization module is installed in the hydraulic reservoir attach bracket set and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the hydraulic reservoir pressurization module:

- Skydrol rapidly degrades eye tissue — goggles are mandatory
- residual pressure in the high-pressure manifold can inject fluid through the skin

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-845 pressure gauge rig — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-508 bonding meter — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-2926-364-A — Nordwind Aero Supply.

### Installation

1. Position the hydraulic reservoir pressurization module on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
2. Torque the hydraulic reservoir pressurization module attach bolts to 78.0 Nm in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the hydraulic reservoir pressurization module and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
4. Restore the hydraulic power generation and distribution circuit supply to the hydraulic reservoir pressurization module and bleed the applicable lines as specified.

### Test and Close-up

1. Energize the hydraulic power generation and distribution test configuration and confirm the hydraulic reservoir pressurization module responds to command inputs.
2. Record the reference value accumulator air pre-charge 1450 psi and compare it with the acceptance band for the hydraulic reservoir pressurization module.
3. Cycle the hydraulic reservoir pressurization module through 5 complete operating cycles and watch for hesitation or drift.
   WARNING: the accumulator pre-charge side stays pressurized with the system depressurized
4. Leak-check every port and coupling touched during this task with the system at operating pressure.
   NOTE: Verify the hydraulic reservoir pressurization module is relieved of stored energy before continuing.
5. Monitor the no external leakage appears at the module ports after pressurization during cycle 01 and log any deviation in the task sheet.
