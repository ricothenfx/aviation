# NX320 AMM · ATA 32 · 32-35-00 · Task 32-35-00-242-227 · nose wheel steering tiller feedback unit — removal and installation
## General

This task card covers the removal and installation of the nose wheel steering tiller feedback unit on the NX-320 landing gear and steering (Landing Gear, ATA 32).
The nose wheel steering tiller feedback unit is installed in the side stay pin attach hardware kit and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
Bench-test operating pressure reference for this card: 1876 bar.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the nose wheel steering tiller feedback unit:

- an unsecured uplock can release the gear under its own weight
- gear doors can fall when their hinges are disconnected without support

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-630 borescope — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-911 generator load bank — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-3211-430-A — Halcyon Hydraulics.

## Procedure

1. Depressurize and drain the landing gear and steering circuit serving the nose wheel steering tiller feedback unit, then verify zero pressure on the associated gauge.
2. Attach the applicable warning placards and open the access panel adjacent to the nose wheel steering tiller feedback unit.
   NOTE: Verify the main gear side stay pin is relieved of pressure before continuing.
3. Disconnect the electrical connector of the nose wheel steering tiller feedback unit and cap the receptacle with a protective cover.

### Installation

1. Position the nose wheel steering tiller feedback unit on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
2. Torque the nose wheel steering tiller feedback unit attach bolts to 45.0 Nm in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the nose wheel steering tiller feedback unit and confirm the latch clicks fully home.

<!-- page: 327 -->
4. Restore the landing gear and steering circuit supply to the nose wheel steering tiller feedback unit and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
   NOTE: Verify the gear actuating cylinder snubber is relieved of residual torque before continuing.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the nose wheel steering tiller feedback unit mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-3211-430-A).
6. Close the access panel and remove the warning placards once the nose wheel steering tiller feedback unit is secured.
7. Reinstall the bracket at position 01 of the nose wheel steering tiller feedback unit installation; torque the coupling per the standard practice table.
