# NX320 AMM · ATA 21 · 21-45-00 · Task 21-45-00-323-378 · temperature trim air valve — operational test
## General

This task card covers the operational test of the temperature trim air valve on the NX-320 air conditioning and pressurization (Air Conditioning, ATA 21).
The temperature trim air valve is installed in the pack bay ducting assembly and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the temperature trim air valve:

- conditioned air ducts can exceed 120 °C during bleed operations
- sudden cabin pressure changes can dislodge unsecured panels

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-508 bonding meter — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-630 borescope — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-2113-539-A — Meridian Gear Co..

### Installation

1. Position the temperature trim air valve on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
2. Torque the temperature trim air valve attach bolts to 44.0 lbf·ft in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
   NOTE: Verify the pack flow control valve is relieved of stored energy before continuing.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the temperature trim air valve and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
4. Restore the air conditioning and pressurization circuit supply to the temperature trim air valve and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the temperature trim air valve mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-2113-539-A).
