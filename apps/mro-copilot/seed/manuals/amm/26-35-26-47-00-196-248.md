# NX320 AMM · ATA 26 · 26-47-00 · Task 26-47-00-196-248 · fire handle unlock solenoid — inspection and check
## General

This task card covers the inspection and check of the fire handle unlock solenoid on the NX-320 fire detection and extinguishing (Fire Protection, ATA 26).
The fire handle unlock solenoid is installed in the extinguisher bottle mounting strap set and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
Bench-test operating pressure reference for this card: 1502 bar.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the fire handle unlock solenoid:

- extinguisher bottles are pressurized — a discharged squib can cause injury
- the detection loop stays armed with the engine OFF

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-845 pressure gauge rig — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-210 torque wrench set — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-2624-363-B — Meridian Gear Co..

### Installation

1. Position the fire handle unlock solenoid on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
   NOTE: extinguisher bottles are pressurized — a discharged squib can cause injury
2. Torque the fire handle unlock solenoid attach bolts to 76.0 Nm in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
   CAUTION: Verify the engine fire detection loop element is relieved of power before continuing.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the fire handle unlock solenoid and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
   NOTE: the detection loop stays armed with the engine OFF
4. Restore the fire detection and extinguishing circuit supply to the fire handle unlock solenoid and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
   NOTE: Verify the fire extinguisher bottle squib is relieved of power before continuing.
