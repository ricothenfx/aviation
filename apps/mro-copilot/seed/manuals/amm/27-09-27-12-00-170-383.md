# NX320 AMM · ATA 27 · 27-12-00 · Task 27-12-00-170-383 · elevator feel and centering unit — inspection and check
## General

This task card covers the inspection and check of the elevator feel and centering unit on the NX-320 primary and secondary flight controls (Flight Controls, ATA 27).
The elevator feel and centering unit is installed in the flap carriage and track assembly and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the elevator feel and centering unit:

- hydraulic pressure in the PCU circuit can cause uncommanded surface movement
- control surface mass balance weights must never be loosened without support

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-845 pressure gauge rig — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-422 hydraulic test rig — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-2743-602-B — Kepler Avionics.

### Installation

1. Position the elevator feel and centering unit on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
2. Torque the elevator feel and centering unit attach bolts to 22.9 lbf·ft in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.

### Test and Close-up

1. Energize the primary and secondary flight controls test configuration and confirm the elevator feel and centering unit responds to command inputs.
2. Record the reference value flap skew margin 1.8° and compare it with the acceptance band for the elevator feel and centering unit.

<!-- page: 211 -->
3. Cycle the elevator feel and centering unit through 5 complete operating cycles and watch for hesitation or drift.
4. Leak-check every port and coupling touched during this task with the system at operating pressure.
5. Monitor the surface travels match the rigging pin reference within 0.5° during cycle 01 and log any deviation in the task sheet.
6. Monitor the the feel force gradient stays within the tolerance band during cycle 02 and log any deviation in the task sheet.
