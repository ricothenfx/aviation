# NX320 AMM · ATA 28 · 28-31-00 · Task 28-31-00-474-260 · center tank fuel pump — inspection and check
## General

This task card covers the inspection and check of the center tank fuel pump on the NX-320 fuel storage and distribution (Fuel, ATA 28).
The center tank fuel pump is installed in the refuel gallery coupling assembly and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the center tank fuel pump:

- residual fuel keeps dripping from couplings for several minutes
- fuel vapour in the tank volume is explosive at ordinary temperatures

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-911 generator load bank — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-314 rigging pin kit — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-2856-189-A — Kepler Avionics.

### Installation

1. Position the center tank fuel pump on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
2. Torque the center tank fuel pump attach bolts to 47.5 Nm in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
   NOTE: Verify the center tank fuel pump is relieved of stored energy before continuing.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the center tank fuel pump and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
4. Restore the fuel storage and distribution circuit supply to the center tank fuel pump and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the center tank fuel pump mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-2856-189-A).
6. Close the access panel and remove the warning placards once the center tank fuel pump is secured.
7. Reinstall the support strut at position 01 of the center tank fuel pump installation; torque the coupling per the standard practice table.
8. Reinstall the vent line at position 02 of the center tank fuel pump installation; torque the coupling per the standard practice table.
