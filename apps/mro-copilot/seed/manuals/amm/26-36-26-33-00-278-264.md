# NX320 AMM · ATA 26 · 26-33-00 · Task 26-33-00-278-264 · fire handle unlock solenoid — removal and installation
## General

This task card covers the removal and installation of the fire handle unlock solenoid on the NX-320 fire detection and extinguishing (Fire Protection, ATA 26).
The fire handle unlock solenoid is installed in the extinguisher bottle mounting strap set and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
Bench-test operating pressure reference for this card: 2195 bar.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the fire handle unlock solenoid:

- the detection loop stays armed with the engine OFF
- extinguisher bottles are pressurized — a discharged squib can cause injury

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-314 rigging pin kit — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-508 bonding meter — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-2686-638-A — Kepler Avionics.

1. Depressurize and drain the fire detection and extinguishing circuit serving the fire handle unlock solenoid, then verify zero pressure on the associated gauge.

### Installation

1. Position the fire handle unlock solenoid on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
   WARNING: the detection loop stays armed with the engine OFF
2. Torque the fire handle unlock solenoid attach bolts to 26.0 Nm in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the fire handle unlock solenoid and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
4. Restore the fire detection and extinguishing circuit supply to the fire handle unlock solenoid and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the fire handle unlock solenoid mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-2686-638-A).
