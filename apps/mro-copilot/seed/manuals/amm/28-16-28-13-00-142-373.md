# NX320 AMM · ATA 28 · 28-13-00 · Task 28-13-00-142-373 · low-pressure fuel shut-off valve — replacement
## General

This task card covers the replacement of the low-pressure fuel shut-off valve on the NX-320 fuel storage and distribution (Fuel, ATA 28).
The low-pressure fuel shut-off valve is installed in the refuel gallery coupling assembly and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the low-pressure fuel shut-off valve:

- fuel vapour in the tank volume is explosive at ordinary temperatures
- residual fuel keeps dripping from couplings for several minutes

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-508 bonding meter — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-777 system test set — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-2810-469-B — Halcyon Hydraulics.

### Installation

1. Position the low-pressure fuel shut-off valve on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
2. Torque the low-pressure fuel shut-off valve attach bolts to 87.5 Nm in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the low-pressure fuel shut-off valve and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
4. Restore the fuel storage and distribution circuit supply to the low-pressure fuel shut-off valve and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
   NOTE: Verify the wing tank water scavenger system is relieved of residual torque before continuing.

   WARNING: residual fuel keeps dripping from couplings for several minutes
