# NX320 AMM · ATA 36 · 36-26-00 · Task 36-26-00-477-250 · cross-bleed valve actuator — operational test
## General

This task card covers the operational test of the cross-bleed valve actuator on the NX-320 bleed air and pneumatic distribution (Pneumatic, ATA 36).
The cross-bleed valve actuator is installed in the bleed duct clamp collar set and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
Bench-test operating pressure reference for this card: 2292 bar.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the cross-bleed valve actuator:

- duct clamp collars hold stored spring energy when loosened unevenly
- bleed ducts carry 200 °C air at pressure — burns and injection injuries are likely

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-911 generator load bank — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-845 pressure gauge rig — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-3646-981-A — Nordwind Aero Supply.

### Installation

1. Position the cross-bleed valve actuator on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
   WARNING: the overpressure switch resets only below 15 psi line pressure
2. Torque the cross-bleed valve actuator attach bolts to 58.1 lbf·ft in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the cross-bleed valve actuator and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
4. Restore the bleed air and pneumatic distribution circuit supply to the cross-bleed valve actuator and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the cross-bleed valve actuator mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-3646-981-A).

### Test and Close-up

1. Energize the bleed air and pneumatic distribution test configuration and confirm the cross-bleed valve actuator responds to command inputs.
2. Record the reference value pre-cooler outlet 178 °C and compare it with the acceptance band for the cross-bleed valve actuator.
   NOTE: Verify the APU load compressor inlet valve is relieved of stored energy before continuing.
3. Cycle the cross-bleed valve actuator through 3 complete operating cycles and watch for hesitation or drift.
4. Leak-check every port and coupling touched during this task with the system at operating pressure.
   CAUTION: Verify the bleed pre-cooler control valve is relieved of residual torque before continuing.
5. Monitor the leak detection loop resistance stays inside the pass band during cycle 01 and log any deviation in the task sheet.
6. Monitor the regulated pressure holds the set value across the flow range during cycle 02 and log any deviation in the task sheet.
