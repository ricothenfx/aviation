# NX320 AMM · ATA 26 · 26-34-00 · Task 26-34-00-220-313 · APU fire extinguishing bottle — removal and installation
## General

This task card covers the removal and installation of the APU fire extinguishing bottle on the NX-320 fire detection and extinguishing (Fire Protection, ATA 26).
The APU fire extinguishing bottle is installed in the cargo smoke detection manifold and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the APU fire extinguishing bottle:

- extinguisher bottles are pressurized — a discharged squib can cause injury
- test filaments stay hot enough to burn skin for a minute after the test

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-422 hydraulic test rig — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-210 torque wrench set — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-2664-509-A — Kepler Avionics.

## Procedure

### Removal

1. Depressurize and drain the fire detection and extinguishing circuit serving the APU fire extinguishing bottle, then verify zero pressure on the associated gauge.
2. Attach the applicable warning placards and open the access panel adjacent to the APU fire extinguishing bottle.
3. Disconnect the electrical connector of the APU fire extinguishing bottle and cap the receptacle with a protective cover.
   NOTE: extinguisher bottles are pressurized — a discharged squib can cause injury
4. Support the APU fire extinguishing bottle with a suitable sling before loosening any attach hardware.

### Installation

1. Position the APU fire extinguishing bottle on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
2. Torque the APU fire extinguishing bottle attach bolts to 84.5 Nm in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the APU fire extinguishing bottle and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
4. Restore the fire detection and extinguishing circuit supply to the APU fire extinguishing bottle and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
   NOTE: Verify the lavatory smoke detector unit is relieved of residual torque before continuing.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the APU fire extinguishing bottle mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-2664-509-A).
6. Close the access panel and remove the warning placards once the APU fire extinguishing bottle is secured.
7. Reinstall the drain line at position 01 of the APU fire extinguishing bottle installation; torque the coupling per the standard practice table.

### Test and Close-up

1. Energize the fire detection and extinguishing test configuration and confirm the APU fire extinguishing bottle responds to command inputs.
2. Record the reference value loop resistance 3.8 Ω and compare it with the acceptance band for the APU fire extinguishing bottle.

3. Cycle the APU fire extinguishing bottle through 3 complete operating cycles and watch for hesitation or drift.
   WARNING: test filaments stay hot enough to burn skin for a minute after the test
