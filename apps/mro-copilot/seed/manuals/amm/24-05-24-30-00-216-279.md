# NX320 AMM · ATA 24 · 24-30-00 · Task 24-30-00-216-279 · main battery contactor — inspection and check
## General

This task card covers the inspection and check of the main battery contactor on the NX-320 electrical power generation and distribution (Electrical Power, ATA 24).
The main battery contactor is installed in the primary power distribution rack and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
Bench-test operating pressure reference for this card: 1911 bar.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the main battery contactor:

- arc-flash PPE is mandatory before opening loaded bus tie panels
- the TRU cooling fins stay hot for several minutes after shutdown

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-777 system test set — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-845 pressure gauge rig — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-2456-211-B — Halcyon Hydraulics.

### Installation

1. Position the main battery contactor on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
2. Torque the main battery contactor attach bolts to 44.5 Nm in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
   NOTE: Verify the variable speed constant frequency generator is relieved of stored energy before continuing.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the main battery contactor and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
4. Restore the electrical power generation and distribution circuit supply to the main battery contactor and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the main battery contactor mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-2456-211-B).

### Test and Close-up

1. Energize the electrical power generation and distribution test configuration and confirm the main battery contactor responds to command inputs.
2. Record the reference value TRU output 28.0 VDC and compare it with the acceptance band for the main battery contactor.
3. Cycle the main battery contactor through 6 complete operating cycles and watch for hesitation or drift.
4. Leak-check every port and coupling touched during this task with the system at operating pressure.
5. Monitor the TRU output ripple stays below the stated limit for 60 s during cycle 01 and log any deviation in the task sheet.
6. Monitor the TRU output ripple stays below the stated limit for 60 s during cycle 02 and log any deviation in the task sheet.
7. Monitor the battery contactor clicks once, without chattering, on engage during cycle 03 and log any deviation in the task sheet.
8. Monitor the generator frequency holds 400 Hz ± 2 Hz under load transfer during cycle 04 and log any deviation in the task sheet.
