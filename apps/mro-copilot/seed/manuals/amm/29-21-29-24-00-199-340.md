# NX320 AMM · ATA 29 · 29-24-00 · Task 29-24-00-199-340 · power transfer unit — inspection and check
## General

This task card covers the inspection and check of the power transfer unit on the NX-320 hydraulic power generation and distribution (Hydraulic Power, ATA 29).
The power transfer unit is installed in the hydraulic reservoir attach bracket set and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the power transfer unit:

- Skydrol rapidly degrades eye tissue — goggles are mandatory
- residual pressure in the high-pressure manifold can inject fluid through the skin

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-210 torque wrench set — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-911 generator load bank — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-2924-784-C — Halcyon Hydraulics.

### Installation

1. Position the power transfer unit on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
   NOTE: the accumulator pre-charge side stays pressurized with the system depressurized
2. Torque the power transfer unit attach bolts to 15.5 Nm in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the power transfer unit and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
4. Restore the hydraulic power generation and distribution circuit supply to the power transfer unit and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the power transfer unit mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-2924-784-C).
6. Close the access panel and remove the warning placards once the power transfer unit is secured.
   WARNING: Verify the engine-driven pump (EDP) is relieved of pressure before continuing.

### Test and Close-up

1. Energize the hydraulic power generation and distribution test configuration and confirm the power transfer unit responds to command inputs.
2. Record the reference value unloaded system decay 120 psi/min and compare it with the acceptance band for the power transfer unit.
3. Cycle the power transfer unit through 4 complete operating cycles and watch for hesitation or drift.
