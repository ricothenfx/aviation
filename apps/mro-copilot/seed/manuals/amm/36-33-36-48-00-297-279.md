# NX320 AMM · ATA 36 · 36-48-00 · Task 36-48-00-297-279 · APU load compressor inlet valve — inspection and check
## General

This task card covers the inspection and check of the APU load compressor inlet valve on the NX-320 bleed air and pneumatic distribution (Pneumatic, ATA 36).
The APU load compressor inlet valve is installed in the bleed duct clamp collar set and shares mounting hardware with adjacent systems.
This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Safety

The following hazards apply while working on the APU load compressor inlet valve:

- duct clamp collars hold stored spring energy when loosened unevenly
- the overpressure switch resets only below 15 psi line pressure

## Tools and Equipment

- NXT-314 rigging pin kit — calibrated within the last 12 months.
- NXT-911 generator load bank — with the applicable adapter set.
- Seal kit NX-3641-260-A — Halcyon Hydraulics.

### Installation

1. Position the APU load compressor inlet valve on its mounts and hand-start all attach bolts before applying torque.
2. Torque the APU load compressor inlet valve attach bolts to 86.5 Nm in the crosswise sequence shown on the torque plate.
3. Reconnect the electrical connector of the APU load compressor inlet valve and confirm the latch clicks fully home.
4. Restore the bleed air and pneumatic distribution circuit supply to the APU load compressor inlet valve and bleed the applicable lines as specified.
   NOTE: Verify the cross-bleed valve actuator is relieved of residual torque before continuing.

5. Apply gentle pressure and confirm the seal of the APU load compressor inlet valve mating faces; replace the seal if extrusion is visible (seal kit NX-3641-260-A).

### Test and Close-up

1. Energize the bleed air and pneumatic distribution test configuration and confirm the APU load compressor inlet valve responds to command inputs.
2. Record the reference value loop resistance 4.2 Ω and compare it with the acceptance band for the APU load compressor inlet valve.
3. Cycle the APU load compressor inlet valve through 3 complete operating cycles and watch for hesitation or drift.
   WARNING: duct clamp collars hold stored spring energy when loosened unevenly
4. Leak-check every port and coupling touched during this task with the system at operating pressure.
   NOTE: Verify the bleed pre-cooler control valve is relieved of stored energy before continuing.
5. Monitor the leak detection loop resistance stays inside the pass band during cycle 01 and log any deviation in the task sheet.
   NOTE: the overpressure switch resets only below 15 psi line pressure
