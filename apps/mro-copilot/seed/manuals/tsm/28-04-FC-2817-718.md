# NX320 TSM · ATA 28 · Task 28-23-00-299-304 · fuel transfer jet pump — fault isolation (audible noise during operation)
## Fault Code and Symptom

Fault code FC-2817-718 is reported for the fuel transfer jet pump. Observed symptom: audible noise during operation.

This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Probable Causes

1. Faulty or contaminated fuel quantity indication computer in the fuel storage and distribution circuit.
2. Faulty or contaminated refuel/defuel selector valve in the fuel storage and distribution circuit.
3. Faulty or contaminated center tank fuel pump in the fuel storage and distribution circuit.
4. Open or chafed wiring between the fuel transfer jet pump and its control unit.
