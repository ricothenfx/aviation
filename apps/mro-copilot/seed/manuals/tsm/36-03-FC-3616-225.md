# NX320 TSM · ATA 36 · Task 36-38-00-428-243 · pressure regulating valve — fault isolation (out-of-range reading on the related display)
## Fault Code and Symptom

Fault code FC-3616-225 is reported for the pressure regulating valve. Observed symptom: out-of-range reading on the related display.

This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Probable Causes

1. Faulty or contaminated APU load compressor inlet valve in the bleed air and pneumatic distribution circuit.
2. Faulty or contaminated overpressure shut-off switch in the bleed air and pneumatic distribution circuit.
3. Faulty or contaminated pressure regulating valve in the bleed air and pneumatic distribution circuit.
4. Open or chafed wiring between the pressure regulating valve and its control unit.
