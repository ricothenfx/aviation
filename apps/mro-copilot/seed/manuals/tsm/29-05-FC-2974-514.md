# NX320 TSM · ATA 29 · Task 29-32-00-337-312 · hydraulic reservoir pressurization module — fault isolation (slow response after start)
## Fault Code and Symptom

Fault code FC-2974-514 is reported for the hydraulic reservoir pressurization module. Observed symptom: slow response after start.

This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Probable Causes

1. Faulty or contaminated electric motor pump (EMP) in the hydraulic power generation and distribution circuit.
2. Faulty or contaminated hydraulic reservoir pressurization module in the hydraulic power generation and distribution circuit.
3. Faulty or contaminated power transfer unit in the hydraulic power generation and distribution circuit.
4. Open or chafed wiring between the hydraulic reservoir pressurization module and its control unit.
