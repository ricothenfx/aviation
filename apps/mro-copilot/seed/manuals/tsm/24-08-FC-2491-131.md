# NX320 TSM · ATA 24 · Task 24-25-00-307-409 · electrical essential bus feed breaker — fault isolation (no output at the related sensor)
## Fault Code and Symptom

Fault code FC-2491-131 is reported for the electrical essential bus feed breaker. Observed symptom: no output at the related sensor.

This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Probable Causes

1. Faulty or contaminated static inverter in the electrical power generation and distribution circuit.
2. Faulty or contaminated variable speed constant frequency generator in the electrical power generation and distribution circuit.
3. Faulty or contaminated main battery contactor in the electrical power generation and distribution circuit.
4. Open or chafed wiring between the electrical essential bus feed breaker and its control unit.
