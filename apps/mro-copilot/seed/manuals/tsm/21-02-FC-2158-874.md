# NX320 TSM · ATA 21 · Task 21-11-00-364-353 · temperature trim air valve — fault isolation (fails to respond to command)
## Fault Code and Symptom

Fault code FC-2158-874 is reported for the temperature trim air valve. Observed symptom: fails to respond to command.

This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Probable Causes

1. Faulty or contaminated water extractor in the air conditioning and pressurization circuit.
2. Faulty or contaminated re-circulation fan in the air conditioning and pressurization circuit.
3. Faulty or contaminated pack flow control valve in the air conditioning and pressurization circuit.
4. Open or chafed wiring between the temperature trim air valve and its control unit.
