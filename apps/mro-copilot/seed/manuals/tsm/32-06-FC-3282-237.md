# NX320 TSM · ATA 32 · Task 32-21-00-226-280 · nose landing gear shock absorber — fault isolation (vibration reported by the crew)
## Fault Code and Symptom

Fault code FC-3282-237 is reported for the nose landing gear shock absorber. Observed symptom: vibration reported by the crew.

This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Probable Causes

1. Faulty or contaminated gear actuating cylinder snubber in the landing gear and steering circuit.
2. Faulty or contaminated nose wheel steering tiller feedback unit in the landing gear and steering circuit.
3. Faulty or contaminated tire pressure sensor unit in the landing gear and steering circuit.
4. Open or chafed wiring between the nose landing gear shock absorber and its control unit.
