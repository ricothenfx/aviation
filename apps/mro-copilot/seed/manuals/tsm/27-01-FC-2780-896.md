# NX320 TSM · ATA 27 · Task 27-47-00-340-248 · rudder trim actuator — fault isolation (intermittent indication)
## Fault Code and Symptom

Fault code FC-2780-896 is reported for the rudder trim actuator. Observed symptom: intermittent indication.

This card belongs to the simulated NX-320 training corpus (fictional aircraft; not for real-world maintenance use).

## Probable Causes

1. Faulty or contaminated flap skew sensor in the primary and secondary flight controls circuit.
2. Faulty or contaminated aileron PCU in the primary and secondary flight controls circuit.
3. Faulty or contaminated pitch trim motor clutch in the primary and secondary flight controls circuit.
4. Open or chafed wiring between the rudder trim actuator and its control unit.
